SurfaceReader for macOS
=======================

SurfaceReader speaks the controls, LEDs and displays of MIDI control surfaces (Mackie Control, Logic Control,
HUI, Mackie C4, Frontier TranzPort and others) through VoiceOver.

Installing
----------
Drag SurfaceReader to the Applications folder.

SurfaceReader is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so the first time you open it macOS asks you to confirm.
Control-click SurfaceReader in Applications and choose Open (with VoiceOver, VO-Shift-M opens the shortcut menu),
then choose Open again. Or, after trying to open it once, use System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.

Where SurfaceReader keeps your files
------------------------------------
Your surfaces, protocols, app configurations and settings are in:
    ~/Library/Application Support/Raised Bar/SurfaceReader
The protocols and app configurations that come with SurfaceReader are copied there the first time it runs.
Ones you have changed are never overwritten.
