Changing the MIDI Hardware Monitoring for Windows

  1. Highlight the control surface whose setup you want to change in the Surfaces list.
  2. Bring up the Surfaces menu then select Properties, followed by Hardware MIDI Setup. A dialog box will appear that allows you to specify the MIDI In and Out ports that will handle the messages transmitted by your control surface’s buttons, etc..
  3. When the dialog box first opens you will be positioned on the MIDI In list, so use your up/down arrow keys to highlight the MIDI In port that your surface is physically attached to.
  4. Press Tab to get to the MIDI Out list and choose the virtual MIDI Out port that represents the other end of the cable you used as the MIDI In within your application (for example, MIDI-Yoke Out 1 if your software’s MIDI In is set to MIDI-Yoke In 1).  This virtual port will pass your surface’s MIDI messages along to your application software.
  5. Press Tab until you get to the OK button and press Enter to leave the dialog box.
