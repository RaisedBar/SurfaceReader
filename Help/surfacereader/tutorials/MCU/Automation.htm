Automation

Many of you know that you can enable the writing of automation data to a track by activating a button with keyboard commands, then move faders and v-pots on your control surface during playback to create that automation data.  But, we can do more than that with the Mackie Control Universal.  Here’s a list of button presses, followed by more explanation.

  • Toggle track volume automation by pressing M1+Arm on the appropriate strip.
  • Toggle track pan automation by pressing M1+V-Pot click for the appropriate strip.
  • Toggle track Mute automation by pressing M1+Mute for the appropriate strip.
  • Toggle parameter automation by assigning the parameter to a V-Pot then press M1+V-Pot click for the assigned V-Pot.
  • Press the Disarm button to disable all automation-enabled parameters.
  • Take an automation snapshot by pressing the Snapshot button.
  • Toggle automation playback on and off with the Read/Off button.
  • The Offset button toggles between offset and envelope mode.  M3+Offset returns all faders to their default values.  M4+Offset sets all V-Pots to their default values.
