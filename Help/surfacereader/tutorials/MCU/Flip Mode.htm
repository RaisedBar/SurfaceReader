Flip Mode

One more feature is worth exploring, before we finish our discussion of Sonar modes.  Flip mode flips the assignments between your faders and v-pots .  Press the Flip button to activate Flip.  The first press duplicates the v-pot assignments on your faders.  The second press of the Flip button swaps assignments, so your faders now control what the v-pots were assigned to, and your v-pots now control what the faders were assigned to.  The third press of the Flip button returns to the default behavior.

Here’s an example.  In track Multi, with the faders normally assigned to track volumes and v-pots assigned to track pans, pressing Flip assigns both v-pots and faders to pans.  The second press of Flip swaps the assignments, so v-pots now control volumes and faders control pans.  The third press of Flip returns things to their default, with faders controlling volume and v-pots controlling pan.  You can activate these duplicate or swap functions by pressing Flip in any of the six Sonar modes, with the results depending on your v-pot assignments.
