Partial Summary

Let’s summarize what we have covered so far.  Use Control Group Tracks to control tracks, Control Group Auxiliary to control busses and Control Group Main to control main outs.  the Edit button takes you in and out of Edit mode.  Use Bank Left and Bank Right to scroll through banks of tracks, busses and parameters.  Use Channel Left and Channel Right to move one item at a time with any of these.  The Track Mode button switches between Multi and Single modes.

NOTE: Mackie Control Universal Assignment display items contain a period “.” When the unit is in Single mode.  For example, when you are controlling tracks in Multi mode, the Assignment display shows “TR”; but when you are in Single mode, there is also a period “TR.”
